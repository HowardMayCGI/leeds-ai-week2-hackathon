## Inspiration
A few weeks ago, on a flight to Sicily, I was sat next to a 72 year old man and we got talking about many things, including AI and how it affects our lives.  He mentioned that being older he had 10 different medications, and it was difficult to know when to take them as some must be with meals, and some can't be taken with others.  He told me he used ChatGPT to create him a personal schedule for which drugs to take when during the day.
On seeing the challenge of the hackathon, and discussing with within the team, we thought this was a great idea, but why keep it limited to enterprising individuals, we can harness the NHS App's knowledge of a patient prescriptions and make it an app feature to schedule you medicines.

## What it does
Extends NHS App to not just list a patient medications, but to allow
the patient to construct a medicines schedule, so they know which medications to take when, during the day.

## How we built it
Once we agreed as a team on which problem we were solving we broke
the work down into key steps, and listed the steps needed on a whiteboard.
Team members picked up tasks they felt confortable working on.
We used the NHS App Design System, with agentic coding.  All the code is
new code written by AI, but guided by us, our knowledge and materials found on the web.

## Challenges we ran into
- Having too many ideas to implement
- Breaking the work down into small steps
- Merging code and resolving conflicts

## Accomplishments that we're proud of
- Getting a slick working prototype

## What we learned
- How the need we found is real
- How quickly we can create working apps with agentic coding

## What's next for Schedule My Medications
If we wanted to progress we would do user research. Look at medicines
reminders. And find a way to show it to the NHS App team.
