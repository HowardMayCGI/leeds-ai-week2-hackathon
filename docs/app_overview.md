Using the NHS App Design System, design a flow for a new “Schedule my medicines” feature. Match the navigation, typography, colours and components of the existing NHS App screens provided.

The feature should help patients who take several medicines organise their doses around their daily routine, including meals and any required gaps between medicines.

First-time user flow:
1. Patient opens the Prescriptions section of the NHS App and selects “Schedule my medicines”.
2. An introduction explains what the feature does and what information the patient will need. Patient selects “Get started”.
3. Patient completes a short “Tell us about your routine” questionnaire, including their usual waking time, bedtime and meal times.
4. Patient checks their medicines. The app displays medicines from their record and asks them to confirm which they currently take, including the dose, frequency and prescribed instructions. They can flag missing or incorrect information.
5. Patient adds other medicines and supplements, including items bought without a prescription, and any additional instructions from their pharmacist or prescriber.
6. Patient reviews their answers and selects “Create my schedule”. The proposed schedule uses confirmed medicine instructions and the patient’s routine to arrange doses, accounting for food requirements and required spacing. If information is missing or requirements conflict, the app explains what needs checking with a pharmacist before completing the schedule.
7. Patient views their proposed schedule, organised by time of day. Each entry shows the medicine, dose, time and relevant instructions, such as taking it with food. The patient can review their routine and request adjustments without overriding prescribed instructions.
8. Patient confirms and saves the schedule.
9. Patient can enable medicine reminders, choose their reminder preferences and grant notification permission. Reminders are optional.
   Returning user flow:
- Patient opens Prescriptions → Schedule my medicines and goes directly to their saved schedule.
- They can view dose details, manage reminders or update their routine and medicine information.
- When information changes, the app guides them through reviewing and confirming an updated schedule.
