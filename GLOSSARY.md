# Schedule my medicines

## Language

**Medicine product**: A particular strength and formulation of a medicine, which can contain more than one active ingredient.

**Active ingredient**: The substance responsible for a medicine's therapeutic effect. Different products can contain the same ingredient.

**Potential interaction**: A combination of medicines that may change effectiveness or increase harm. A recorded combination does not establish that harm has occurred or that the medicines must never be prescribed together.
_Avoid_: Conflict, incompatibility

**Dose separation**: A required time gap between doses that can reduce a particular interaction. It does not resolve every risk of concurrent treatment.

**Prescriber review**: Assessment of whether concurrent treatment is appropriate, including any dose changes or monitoring. Changing dose times alone cannot replace this assessment.

**Administration requirement**: A condition for taking a particular medicine, such as its relationship to food, other medicines or body posture. It is separate from the prescribed amount and frequency.

**Administration preference**: A recommended way to take a medicine that is distinct from a mandatory administration requirement.

**Interval anchor**: A known dose date or time that establishes when subsequent doses are due for an interval-based regimen.
